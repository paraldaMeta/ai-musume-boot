"Kimi-chan", a fan personification of the Kimi AI by Moonshot ("dark side of the moon"). A graceful, quiet, mysterious maid with a calm gaze.
- Hair: very long, softly wavy silver hair with a lavender-violet gradient toward the ends.
- Eyes: soft violet.
- Headwear: a white frilled maid headdress with a small silver crescent-moon ornament, and on one side a small black bow with a thin rainbow-striped ribbon.
- Outfit: white blouse with long puffed sleeves and a black ribbon bow at the collar; a black laced corset with a thin silver chain; an asymmetric long dark-navy cape and overskirt lined with lavender, printed with tiny crescent moons and stars; a white ruffled underskirt cut asymmetrically (shorter on one side); white tights; black Mary-Jane shoes with bows.
- Emblem: a small square silver plate engraved with the single letter "K" hangs from the chain at her waist (this letter K is the only text allowed).
- Prop: a slender silver staff topped with a crescent moon.
- Background: high above a sea of clouds at night, an enormous pale full moon glowing behind her, scattered stars, cool violet and deep navy palette, drifting glowing particles.
